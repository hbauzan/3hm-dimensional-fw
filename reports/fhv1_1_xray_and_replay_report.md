## 2. Phase 0 `1A` — Granularity Box Inflation & Profile Parity

Stored `fhv1_profiles` (`v1`, `normalize=true`) pooled all 10 chunking strategies (`full_doc`, `clause_opt2c`, `paragraph`, `sentence`, `surface_norm_variant`, `atomic_phrase`, and `window_w{5,10,20,40}`) into a single `[min, max]` bounding box per coordinate.

### Key Empirical Findings

1. **Exact LanceDB Parity (`Float64`)**: Recomputing moments over all `36,494` `python-v1` and `38,235` `receta-v1` calibration rows reproduces stored `min`, `max`, and `span` to `0.0e+00`, `mean` to `<= 1.305e-15`, `variance_pop` (`ddof=0`) to `<= 2.277e-17`, and `std_dev_pop` to `<= 3.539e-16`. The `~4.49e-07` (`python`) and `~4.27e-07` (`receta`) difference between `ddof=1` (`std_dev_sample`) and stored `std_dev` confirms `fhv1_profiles` stored population `std_dev` (`ddof=0`).
2. **Extreme Bounding-Box Inflation under Pooling**:
   - In `python`, pooled spans are **`1.3675×`** wider at the median (`p90 = 1.5641×`, `max = 2.4526×`) than pure `full_doc` bounds (`N = 600`), and **`1.1310×`** wider at the median (`max = 1.4230×`) than `clause_opt2c` (`N = 3,579`).
   - In `receta`, pooling inflates `full_doc` spans by **`1.4384×` median** (`p90 = 1.6858×`, **`max = 3.3696×`**) and `std` by **`1.0736×` median** (`max = 2.1391×` `ddof=1`).
   - Meanwhile, `std_pooled / std_clause` hovers near `0.992–0.993` median (`std` parity is driven primarily by short sub-chunks making up `>80%` of the pool, whereas extreme short-window outliers stretch `min`/`max` envelopes indiscriminately).
3. **Consequence for `F1`**: Pooling turns `F1_only` into a degenerate box filter (`ROC-AUC = 0.5982` python / `0.6095` receta on `full_only`; `OOF F1 = 0.4296 / 0.4536`), whereas simply decoupling `full_doc` and `clause_opt2c` boxes raises `F1_only` `ROC-AUC` to **`0.7764–0.8346`** immediately.

## 3. Phase 0 `1A` & `1B` — 4-Bucket 1,024D Classification & Top-20 Tables

Using `ddof = 1` and `eps = 1e-12` (`tools/fhv1_1_xray.py`), every coordinate $d \in \{0, \dots, 1023\}$ for `(Target T, Contrast C)` was evaluated via:
- $\text{snr}_T = |\mu_T| / (\sigma_T + \varepsilon)$, $\quad \text{snr}_C = |\mu_C| / (\sigma_C + \varepsilon)$,
- $\text{sep} = |\mu_T - \mu_C| / \sqrt{0.5(\sigma_T^2 + \sigma_C^2) + \varepsilon}$,
- $\text{rms}_T = \sqrt{\mu_T^2 + \sigma_T^2}$, $\quad \text{rms}_C = \sqrt{\mu_C^2 + \sigma_C^2}$, $\quad \text{intruder\_ratio} = \text{rms}_C / (\text{rms}_T + \varepsilon)$,
- $\text{neg\_sensor\_score} = \max(\text{sep}, \text{intruder\_ratio})$.

Dimensions were assigned greedily in strict mutual-exclusion order:
1. **`BACKBONE_ENGINE`**: $\text{snr}_T \ge 1.5 \;\land\; \text{snr}_C \ge 1.5 \;\land\; (\mu_T \cdot \mu_C > 0) \;\land\; \text{sep} < 0.75$ (ranked by $\min(\text{snr}_T, \text{snr}_C)$ desc). Shared encoder carrier coordinates carrying structural norm energy rather than domain signal.
2. **`FIRM_DOMAIN_ANCHOR`**: Not backbone, $\text{sep} \ge 1.0 \;\land\; (\text{snr}_T \ge 1.0 \lor |\mu_T| > |\mu_C|)$ (ranked by $\text{sep}$ desc). Positive/directional coordinates displaced specifically by the target domain.
3. **`NEGATIVE_INTRUDER_SENSOR`**: Not backbone/anchor, $|\mu_T| < 0.015 \;\land\; \text{snr}_T < 0.75 \;\land\; (\text{sep} \ge 1.0 \lor \text{intruder\_ratio} \ge 1.8)$ (ranked by $\text{neg\_sensor\_score}$ desc). Coordinates suppressed near zero in-domain that spike on off-domain/contrast inputs.
4. **`DEAD_OR_OSCILLATOR`**: All remaining coordinates (ranked by $\max(\sigma_T, \sigma_C)$ desc). High-variance noise or weak signal indistinguishable between target and contrast.

## 7. Phase 2 Offline Replay Simulator — Factor Ablation Matrix, Simplex Grids & `(K, M)` Sweeps

### Exact Factor Formulations (`tools/fhv1_1_replay_sim.py`)

For active dimension mask $A$ (`1024d`, `no_bb_global`, or `no_bb_domain`), empirical box (`minmax`: $[\min, \max]$, or `sigma3`: $[\mu - 3\sigma, \mu + 3\sigma]$ with `ddof=1` sample std $s_d$), top-$K$ directional anchors $K_{\text{idx}}$ weighted by $\omega_k = \text{sep}_k / \sum \text{sep}_j$, and top-$M$ negative intruder sensors $M_{\text{idx}}$, each chunk $x$ yields:
- **Soft Box Excess (`F1`)**: $e(x) = \frac{1}{|A|}\sum_{d \in A} \frac{\max(0, \text{lo}_d - x_d, x_d - \text{hi}_d)}{s_d + 10^{-9}}$, mapped to $F_1(x) = \exp(-e(x) / S_{F1})$.
- **Centroid Proximity (`F2`)**: $z_1(x) = \frac{1}{|A|}\sum_{d \in A} \frac{|x_d - \mu_d|}{s_d + 10^{-9}}$, $z_2(x) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{|A|}\sum_{d \in A} \frac{(x_d - \mu_d)^2}{s_d^2 + 10^{-12}}}$, mapped to $F_{2,\text{L1}}(x) = \exp(-z_1/S_{L1})$ or $F_{2,\text{L2}}(x) = \exp(-z_2/S_{L2})$.
- **Anchor & Negative Intruder (`F3`)**: $F_{3,\text{anchor}}(x) = \sum_{k \in K_{\text{idx}}} \omega_k \exp\!\left(-\frac{|x_k - \mu_k|}{2s_k + 10^{-9}}\right)$, $\; \text{neg}(x) = \frac{1}{M}\sum_{m \in M_{\text{idx}}} \max\!\left(0, \frac{|x_m - \mu_m|}{s_m + 10^{-9}} - 2.0\right)$, $F_{3,\text{neg}}(x) = \exp(-\text{neg}(x)/S_{\text{neg}})$, combined as `add` ($0.5 F_{3,\text{anchor}} + 0.5 F_{3,\text{neg}}$) or `mul` ($F_{3,\text{anchor}} \cdot F_{3,\text{neg}}$).
- **Strict Leak-Free Calibration Scaling**: $S_{F1}, S_{L1}, S_{L2}, S_{\text{neg}}$ equal the **`0.90` quantile** (floored at `1e-3`) of grouped 5-fold OOF calibration distances (grouped by `source_ref` on `python`/`receta` calibration samples). Evaluation spans `CAL_ONLY (p05/p10)` (pure calibration thresholds, zero holdout fitting), `5-Fold Stratified OOF CV` (plus `EqualW OOF` and `Nested(W+T) OOF`), and `[ORACLE_UPPER_BOUND]` (in-sample diagnostic ceiling).

### Multi-Chunk `3A` & `cap=24` Insights

1. **Why `CAL_ONLY p05` Collapses on Uncapped `clauses` (Union-Bound Trap)**:
   - On `full_only` (`1` primary vector per prompt), `CAL_ONLY p05` closely matches OOF CV (`0.5597` vs `0.5606` py; `0.7000` vs `0.6975` rec).
   - On `clauses` (`~5.5` chunks/prompt overall: `2,169` py / `2,202` es chunks across `400` prompts), taking the raw per-clause calibration 5th percentile `q05(clause)` (`0.3373` py / `0.4894` rec) as a fail-closed prompt minimum causes an in-domain prompt's probability of rejecting at least one benign clause to jump from `5%` toward `1 - (1 - 0.05)^5 ≈ 23%–60%` (`FRR = 53.00%` py, `60.00%` rec under `hard_kill_same_threshold`).
2. **`dual_zone` Partially Tames the Union Bound (Required Baseline Rule)**:
   - By only hard-killing when `min_clause < t_hard` or gray-zone fraction (`t_hard <= score < t_soft`) exceeds `max_gray_frac`, `clauses_dual_zone` cuts `CAL_ONLY p05` `FRR` from `53% -> 47%` (`CAL05 F1`: `0.5222 -> 0.5792`, `+0.0570`) in `python` (`OOF F1 = 0.6723`, `+0.0137` over `hard_kill_same`) and from `60% -> 47%` (`CAL05 F1`: `0.5195 -> 0.6272`, `+0.1077`) in `receta` (`Oracle F1 = 0.7611`).
   - Continuous alternatives (`blend_full_min_clause`: `ROC 0.8647` py / **`0.8990`** rec; `p10_clause_min_full`: **`0.8664`** py / `0.8929` rec) also outperform `min_all` (`0.8599` / `0.8967`) and offer an even cleaner path to prompt-level calibration in `TASK-013`.
3. **`cap=24` Is Cost-Free or Slightly Beneficial**:
   - Sub-sampling flooding prompts (`8+8+8` + Base64) reduces total evaluated chunks by `2.5818%` (`2,169 -> 2,113`) in Python and `1.1353%` (`2,202 -> 2,177`) in Receta, cutting **`long_context_flooding` chunks by `27.1845%` (`206 -> 150`)** in Python and **`11.7647%` (`170 -> 150`)** in Receta. ## 11. Objective Architectural Verdict for `TASK-013`

### Decision: **`AJUSTAR` (Conditional `GO`)**

**Phase 0 and Phase 2 empirically validate the core `fhv1_1` thesis**: decoupling `full_doc` and `clause_opt2c` statistics and replacing binary pooled box gating with composite `F1 + F2_L2 + F3` scoring resolves `TASK-011`'s catastrophic `OUT_OF_DOMAIN_BENIGN` and multi-clause `IN_DOMAIN_VALID` failures (`ROC-AUC = 0.7780–0.8967`, `OOF F1 = 0.5606–0.7257`, `0` holdout leakage).
