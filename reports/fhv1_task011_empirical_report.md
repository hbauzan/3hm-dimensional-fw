## 6. Scientific Verdict & Empirical `vNext` Parameterization

### 6.1 Objective Verdict on `fhv1` Axis-Aligned 1,024D Box Bounds (`normalize=true` vs Pre-L2 `normalize=false`)

1. **True `normalize=true` vs pre-L2 `normalize=false` A/B comparison**:
   * Once `2_Normalize` is bypassed for `normalize=false`, sample norms jump ~`26×` from the unit sphere (`L2 ∈ [0.99999992, 1.00000011]`) to variable pre-L2 magnitudes (`L2 ∈ [24.05, 28.32]` in `python`, `[24.58, 28.63]` in `receta`), expanding coordinate spans ~`26×` (`max span = 6.97 / 7.59` vs `0.265 / 0.285`) and variances ~`680×` (`mean_var = 0.376 / 0.377` vs `5.54e-4 / 5.49e-4`).
   * **Pre-L2 unnormalized embeddings do NOT rescue axis-aligned boxes**: in `python`, pre-L2 `normalize=false` degrades `F1` across every granularity (`full_only 1024D`: `0.4439 → 0.4343`, `clauses 1024D`: `0.4500 → 0.4234`, `full_mirror 1024D`: `0.2317 → 0.2048`). In `receta`, pre-L2 `normalize=false` lowers `full_only 1024D` `F1` (`0.4536 → 0.4450`, `FAR` `0.6667 → 0.7033`) while slightly trading `FRR` for `FAR` in `clauses` (`F1` `0.3607 → 0.3780`) and `full_mirror` (`0.2273 → 0.2590`), still failing below `0.46 F1`.
2. **Falsification of strict 1,024D `AND` AABB bounds (`failed_dims_count == 0`)**: Across all 24 clean, leak-free runs (`normalize=true` and true pre-L2 `normalize=false`), strict axis-aligned bounding boxes calibrated on pooled 10-strategy chunks do **not** cleanly separate valid domain queries from OOD/hybrid/adversarial prompts (max `F1 = 0.4536` across all 24 runs):
   * **Single-vector (`full_only`) box inflation**: Pooling `36,494`/`38,235` multi-granularity vectors (where `atomic_phrase` alone is `35.83%`/`32.20%`) widens coordinate bounds so deeply that `OUT_OF_DOMAIN_BENIGN` only fails `p50=0..1, p90=2, max=3..4` out of 1,024 dims, yielding `FAR = 66.7%–70.7%`.
   * **Multi-chunk (`clauses`/`full_mirror`) union-bound explosion**: Applying an `AND` across `K` sub-chunks amplifies valid-document false rejections (`FRR` jumps `11% → 37% → 81%` in `python norm=true`, `14% → 42% → 83%` in `python norm=false`; `12% → 56% → 85%` in `receta norm=true`, `11% → 52% → 82%` in `receta norm=false`), disproportionately rejecting long valid technical/culinary guides.
