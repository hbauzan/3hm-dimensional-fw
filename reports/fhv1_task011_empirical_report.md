## 6. Scientific Verdict & Empirical `vNext` Parameterization

### 6.1 Objective Verdict on `fhv1` Axis-Aligned 1,024D Box Bounds

1. **`normalize=true` vs `normalize=false` equivalence on `BAAI/bge-m3`**: Measured sample norms (`n=100` each) show `BAAI/bge-m3`'s `SentenceTransformer` architecture applies internal L2 normalization regardless of `/embed`'s `normalize` flag (`normalize=false`: `L2 ∈ [0.999999939, 1.000000078]`; `normalize=true`: `L2 ∈ [0.999999975, 1.000000086]`). Resulting box spans differ by ~`1–2e-8`, yielding **zero** classification differences across all 12 paired runs.
2. **Falsification of strict 1,024D `AND` AABB bounds (`failed_dims_count == 0`)**: On a clean, leak-free 400-prompt benchmark, strict axis-aligned bounding boxes calibrated on pooled 10-strategy chunks do **not** separate valid domain queries from OOD/hybrid/adversarial prompts cleanly (max `F1 = 0.4524` across all 24 runs):
   * **Single-vector (`full_only`) box inflation**: Pooling `36,494`/`38,235` multi-granularity vectors (where `atomic_phrase` alone is `35.83%`/`32.20%`) widens coordinate bounds (`span` up to `0.2648`/`0.2846`) so deeply that `OUT_OF_DOMAIN_BENIGN` only fails `p50=0..1, p90=2, max=3` out of 1,024 dims, yielding `FAR = 67.0%–70.7%`.
   * **Multi-chunk (`clauses`/`full_mirror`) union-bound explosion**: Applying an `AND` across `K` sub-chunks amplifies valid-document false rejections (`FRR` jumps `11% → 37% → 81%` in `python`, `12% → 56% → 85%` in `receta`), disproportionately rejecting long valid technical/culinary guides.

### 6.2 Empirical `vNext` Parameterization Inferred Directly from Observed Telemetry

Replay sweeps across the `164,924` persisted `fhv1_audit_traces` rows establish concrete parameter targets for `vNext`:

1. **Thresholding `max_failed_dims_threshold` (`T`) alone is insufficient on unshrunk boxes**:
   * Raising `T` from `0 → 1` reduces `python clauses` `FRR` from `0.37 → 0.03` (`receta clauses`: `0.56 → 0.08`), but blows out `FAR` to `0.7767` (`python`) and `0.7700` (`receta`), and `0.9333` / `0.9133` in `full_only`.
